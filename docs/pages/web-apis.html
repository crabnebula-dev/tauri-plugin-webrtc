Web APIs

The shim installs the WebRTC classes on window with W3C shapes and event ordering. This page lists what each one does and where it differs from a browser.

Installed classes

RTCPeerConnection (also as webkitRTCPeerConnection), RTCSessionDescription, RTCIceCandidate, RTCDataChannel, RTCPeerConnectionIceEvent, RTCDataChannelEvent, RTCTrackEvent, RTCRtpSender, RTCRtpReceiver, RTCRtpTransceiver, RTCDTMFSender, RTCDTMFToneChangeEvent and RTCRtpScriptTransform. Worker is wrapped so that transform workers get RTCRtpScriptTransformer.

Configuration

iceServers (stun:, turn: with ?transport=udp|tcp, turns:), iceTransportPolicy and bundlePolicy behave as in browsers. One member is not W3C: postQuantum, on Post-quantum.

RTCDataChannel

  • ordered, maxRetransmits, maxPacketLifeTime, protocol, negotiated with id.
  • send() takes strings, ArrayBuffer, typed arrays and Blobs; binaryType is arraybuffer or blob.
  • bufferedAmount, bufferedAmountLowThreshold and bufferedamountlow.

Senders, receivers and transceivers

  • direction, currentDirection, stop(), mid.
  • RTCRtpSender.replaceTrack() swaps the source without renegotiation.
  • getParameters and setParameters: active, maxBitrate and scaleResolutionDownBy apply to the encoder.
  • setCodecPreferences() reorders and filters codecs; getCapabilities(kind) lists what the page can encode.
  • RTCRtpSender.dtmf on audio senders.

Codecs

Audio is Opus. Video is VP8 always, and H.264 when WebCodecs in the webview can encode and decode it; the shim probes that before the first offer. Encoding runs in the page, so the webview's WebCodecs decides what is available.

Simulcast

A sender given several sendEncodings sends one layer: the active encoding with the least downscaling. SFUs such as LiveKit accept that and forward it to every subscriber.

RTCDTMFSender

insertDTMF(tones, duration, interToneGap), toneBuffer, canInsertDTMF and tonechange, as in the spec. Tones go out as RFC 4733 telephone events; a comma pauses for two seconds.

Encoded transforms

RTCRtpScriptTransform, the WebKit and Firefox shape of insertable streams, on senders and receivers for audio and video. Construct it with a Worker; the worker receives rtctransform events with readable and writable streams of RTCEncodedVideoFrame and RTCEncodedAudioFrame. LiveKit's end-to-end encryption runs through it unchanged.

Under a CSP that forbids blob: workers, the shim withdraws RTCRtpScriptTransform instead of letting media stall. See CSP and frames.

Not yet

  • VP9 and AV1.
  • Receiving simulcast layers.
  • Changing ICE servers on a live connection.
  • RTCPeerConnection.generateCertificate() and the certificates configuration member.
  • The Chromium-style createEncodedStreams(); use RTCRtpScriptTransform.
